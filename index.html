Day-by-Day Itinerary

Meet your professional Dream Island Travels & Tours chauffeur at Bandaranaike International Airport. Transfer directly to your hotel in Mirissa. Enjoy a relaxing day at the hotel after your journey.

Overnight Stay At: Mirissa

After breakfast, travel to the Madu River and enjoy a relaxing boat safari through the beautiful mangroves. Continue to Galle Fort, explore its historic streets, and discover its rich history. Later, visit some beautiful beaches along the southern coast and enjoy the seaside atmosphere. In the evening, return to your hotel in Mirissa.

Overnight Stay At: Mirissa

After breakfast, travel from Mirissa to the beautiful Sinharaja Rainforest. Enjoy a guided nature walk through this UNESCO World Heritage Site, exploring its rich biodiversity, tropical plants, and beautiful natural surroundings. After spending time in the rainforest, return to your hotel in Mirissa.

Overnight Stay At: Mirissa

In the morning, enjoy an optional whale and dolphin watching tour to see blue whales and dolphins in their natural habitat. In the evening, relax and enjoy the beautiful beach atmosphere in Mirissa.

Alternatively, you can visit Weligama Beach and enjoy a relaxing beach experience.

Overnight Stay At: Mirissa

Early morning, depart from Mirissa and travel to Udawalawe National Park. Enjoy an exciting morning jeep safari and experience Sri Lanka's beautiful wildlife in its natural habitat. Later, visit the Udawalawe Elephant Transit Home to learn about elephant conservation and rehabilitation. Continue your scenic journey to Ella, stopping at the beautiful Ravana Falls along the way to enjoy the natural surroundings and take photos. Arrive in Ella in the evening and check in to your hotel.

Overnight Stay At: Ella

After breakfast, visit the peaceful Mahamevnawa Buddhist Monastery. Experience the calm and spiritual atmosphere, learn about Buddhist culture and traditions, and enjoy the beautiful natural surroundings. After the visit, return to your hotel in Ella.

Overnight Stay At: Ella

Early in the morning, hike to Little Adam’s Peak and enjoy the beautiful sunrise with breathtaking mountain views. After the hike, return to your hotel for breakfast and some relaxing time. In the afternoon, visit the famous Nine Arch Bridge, enjoy the beautiful scenery, and take memorable photos. Return to your hotel in Ella.

Overnight Stay At: Ella

Enjoy a relaxing morning at your hotel. Later, explore Ella town, walk along its charming streets, visit local shops, enjoy a little shopping, and experience the friendly atmosphere of this beautiful mountain town. Spend the rest of the day at your own pace.

Overnight Stay At: Ella

Early in the morning, travel from Ella to the beautiful Lipton’s Seat viewpoint. Enjoy a scenic journey through some of Sri Lanka’s most beautiful tea plantations. For the final part of the journey, take a local tuk-tuk ride through the charming tea estate roads to reach Lipton’s Seat. Enjoy breathtaking panoramic views of the surrounding tea-covered mountains and experience the beauty of Sri Lanka’s world-famous tea country.

Return to Ella after this memorable experience.

Overnight Stay At: Ella

After breakfast, enjoy one of the most beautiful train journeys in the world, travelling from Ella to Nanu Oya. Experience breathtaking views of lush green tea plantations, misty mountains, beautiful valleys, and charming villages along the way.

Upon arrival at Nanu Oya Railway Station, meet your Dream Island Travels & Tours chauffeur and continue your journey towards Colombo via Nuwara Eliya, a beautiful hill town known for its British colonial heritage and charming atmosphere.

Along the way, explore Nuwara Eliya and visit a traditional tea factory to discover the secrets of Ceylon Tea production. Enjoy a tea-making experience and taste some of Sri Lanka’s world-famous Ceylon Tea.

Later, visit the beautiful Ramboda Falls and enjoy the stunning natural scenery before continuing your journey to Colombo.

Arrive in Colombo and check in to your hotel.

Overnight Stay At: Colombo
